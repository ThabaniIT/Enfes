About ENFES

Eswatini National Fire and Emergency Services

Dedicated to protecting lives, property, and the environment through professional emergency response and prevention services.

ENFES is Eswatini's premier emergency response organization, operating under the Ministry of Public Works and Transport. With strategically located stations across the country, our highly trained teams are ready to respond 24/7 to fires, medical emergencies, hazardous materials incidents, and other disasters.

Beyond emergency response, we are committed to public education, fire prevention, and community preparedness to build a safer Eswatini for all citizens.

Fire Suppression Services
Overview

Our fire suppression teams are trained to respond to all types of fires, from small residential incidents to large industrial conflagrations. We utilize modern equipment and tactics to extinguish fires quickly while minimizing damage and environmental impact.

When to Contact
  • Any visible fire or flames
  • Smoke inside a building
  • Smoke alarms activating
  • Strong burning smell with no visible source
  • Suspected gas leaks or chemical fires
Our Response
  1. Emergency call received and dispatched
  2. First responders arrive within target time frames
  3. Scene assessment and tactical planning
  4. Fire suppression operations
  5. Search and rescue if needed
  6. Overhaul and mop-up operations
  7. Scene investigation and documentation
Equipment and Expertise

ENFES maintains modern pumpers, ladder trucks, and specialized equipment. Our firefighters are trained in structural, vehicle, wildland, and industrial firefighting techniques, with specialized teams for high-risk scenarios.

Fire Safety Information

Protect your family and property by following these essential home fire prevention measures:

  • Install smoke alarms on every level of your home
  • Test smoke alarms monthly and replace batteries annually
  • Keep portable heaters at least 1 meter from anything that can burn
  • Never leave cooking unattended
  • Keep matches and lighters away from children
  • Have your electrical systems inspected by qualified professionals
  • Develop and practice a home escape plan with all family members
Download Home Safety Checklist

Remember the acronym PASS when using a fire extinguisher:

  • Pull the pin while holding the extinguisher away from you.
  • Aim low at the base of the fire.
  • Squeeze the handle slowly and evenly.
  • Sweep the nozzle from side to side at the base of the fire.

Remember: Only use fire extinguishers on small, contained fires. If the fire grows or you feel unsafe, evacuate immediately and call emergency services.

Having a well-practiced escape plan can be the difference between life and death during a fire emergency:

  1. Draw a floor plan of your home showing all possible exits from each room
  2. Mark two ways out of each room if possible
  3. Designate a meeting spot outside the home where everyone should gather
  4. Ensure everyone knows how to call emergency services
  5. Practice your escape plan at least twice a year, both day and night
Remember
  • Feel doors before opening - if hot, find another exit
  • Stay low to avoid smoke inhalation
  • Once out, stay out - never re-enter a burning building
Download Escape Plan Template Schedule a Home Safety Visit

Electrical fires are common but preventable. Follow these guidelines to reduce risk:

Wiring & Outlets
  • Replace damaged electrical cords immediately
  • Avoid running cords under carpets or furniture
  • Don't overload outlets or extension cords
  • Use surge protectors for electronic equipment
Appliances & Equipment
  • Unplug appliances when not in use
  • Keep appliances away from water sources
  • Have regular inspections by qualified electricians
  • Use the correct wattage light bulbs for fixtures
If you smell burning plastic, see sparks, or experience frequent circuit breaker trips, contact a qualified electrician immediately.

Dry Season (May-October)
  • Clear dry vegetation around homes
  • Dispose of cigarettes properly
  • Follow all burn bans and restrictions
  • Keep BBQ grills away from structures
  • Maintain a water source nearby when cooking outdoors
Wet Season (November-April)
  • Keep portable heaters 1m from combustibles
  • Never use stoves for heating purposes
  • Have chimneys and heating systems inspected
  • Use candles only on stable surfaces
  • Never leave heat sources unattended
